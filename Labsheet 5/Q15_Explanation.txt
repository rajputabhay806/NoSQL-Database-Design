Q15 — Neo4j Graph Database Application
Application: a small social network.
Node: Person(person_id, name, city).
Relationship: FRIENDS_WITH.
Q15.py demonstrates create/upsert (MERGE), read (MATCH), update (SET), delete (DETACH DELETE), and relationship traversal.
Graph databases make connected-data queries natural. Neo4j transactions provide atomic commit/rollback within a transaction. Availability and scaling depend on edition, topology, workload, and configuration; a single local instance is not highly available by itself.
